## Problem Statement
CombatTimer (v1.0.2, iOS/Android via Expo) is a shipped multi-discipline round timer for combat sports (boxing, MMA, BJJ, wrestling, kickboxing, Muay Thai) and general conditioning. Monetization today is ads-only: an AdMob banner pinned to Timer, Workout, and Settings screens, plus a full-screen interstitial fired after every completed workout. There is no IAP, no "remove ads" option, no rewarded ad unit, and no subscription — the entire revenue stack is two AdMob units. The goal of this session was to find the highest-leverage way to improve monetization.

## Demand Evidence
None yet. No retention data, no App Store reviews, no repeat-usage signal was cited. The user confirmed this directly ("I don't have that evidence yet"). This is the single biggest risk to any revenue plan — optimizing monetization compounds whatever traffic already exists, but doesn't create traffic.

## Status Quo
Combat-sports users currently solve round-timing with (a) generic HIIT/Tabata interval timer apps not built for combat sports, and (b) gym wall clocks or existing dedicated boxing-bell apps/hardware. Both were confirmed as real, currently-used alternatives.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
No sharp wedge exists. The user initially proposed "a specific discipline" as the differentiator, but the actual preset data (`src/data/presets.ts`) spans boxing, MMA, BJJ, wrestling, kickboxing, Muay Thai, and conditioning — a generalist tool, not a niched one. This was surfaced and the user agreed (Premise #2). No narrowest-wedge was defined in this session; differentiation, if any, has to come from execution/UX, not audience targeting.

## Constraints
- Existing stack: Expo/React Native 0.81, `react-native-google-mobile-ads` already integrated via `src/ads/index.ts` with an Expo Go mock fallback.
- No IAP library installed yet (no `react-native-iap` / `expo-in-app-purchases` in package.json).
- Firebase Analytics is wired up (`@react-native-firebase/analytics`) but not currently driving any monetization decisions.

## Premises
1. CombatTimer has no proven demand yet — optimizing ad revenue on near-zero traffic won't move real dollars until distribution improves. **Agreed.**
2. The product is a generalist multi-discipline timer, not niched — differentiation has to come from UX/execution, not a claimed niche. **Agreed.**
3. Ads-only is currently the entire monetization stack — no IAP, no "remove ads," no rewarded ads — leaving real ARPU on the table even at current traffic. **Agreed.**

## Landscape Notes
- Several competing round-timer apps (e.g. "Boxing Timer Pro," Shadow Boxing) market themselves specifically as **free / no ads** — ads-only is not a differentiator in this category, and could be a competitive disadvantage on reviews if overdone.
- Rewarded video ads pay roughly **10–20x** banner eCPM ($15–30 vs $0.50–1.50 in Tier 1 markets) — CombatTimer currently captures none of that.
- 2026 industry default for non-gaming apps is subscription-first, but the standard advice is to validate demand before adding subscription complexity — supports deferring subscription work here.
- AdMob's own guidance: interstitials belong at natural transition points, not spammed. CombatTimer's current placement (once, after workout completion) already follows this — that part doesn't need fixing.

## Approaches Considered
### Approach A: Fill the Gaps
Keep the current ad setup as-is. Add a rewarded ad unit — resolved use case: "watch an ad to skip the post-workout interstitial for your next session" — and a one-time "Remove Ads" IAP. Effort: S-M (revised up from initial S — see Feasibility Note below). Risk: Low-Medium. Reuses `src/ads/index.ts`, `src/config/adConfig.ts`, and the existing AdMob account/unit structure; only new UI is a Settings toggle plus a "Restore Purchases" control (see Dependencies).

**Feasibility note (from adversarial review):** "no new screens required" understated the real scope. IAP integration is more than a Settings toggle — it requires App Store Connect / Play Console product setup, sandbox testing on both platforms, and a mandatory Restore Purchases flow (Apple rejects non-consumable IAP submissions without one). Budget for this as its own sub-task, not a rider on the ad-unit work.

### Approach B: Freemium Pro Tier
Split the product into free (ads + core presets) and a one-time "CombatTimer Pro" unlock (no ads + custom sound packs + workout history/stats + unlimited saved templates). Effort: M-L. Risk: Medium — real IAP infra, paywall UX, and feature-gating scope; a wrong bet if demand stays unproven. Rejected for now as too much scope for unvalidated traffic. Reuses `useWorkouts.ts`, `bellSounds.ts`, `TemplateScreen.tsx`.

### Approach C: Prove Demand First, Monetize Later
Defer monetization changes entirely; spend the next cycle on ASO/distribution (e.g. niche App Store keywords, review prompts, retention tracking) before touching revenue architecture. Rejected — doesn't address the user's actual ask (a direct monetization improvement) and the marginal cost of Approach A is low enough to do in parallel with any distribution work.

## Recommended Approach
**Approach A: Fill the Gaps.** It's the smallest, lowest-risk change that closes the most obvious hole in the current setup (zero rewarded-ad revenue, zero direct-pay option) without betting real engineering time on a product tier the app hasn't earned yet given unproven demand.

## Success Criteria
- Rewarded ad unit live and integrated through the existing `src/ads/index.ts` abstraction (with Expo Go mock parity). Once "Remove Ads" is purchased, the rewarded-ad skip offer no longer appears (there's nothing left for it to skip).
- "Remove Ads" one-time IAP purchasable, with purchase state validated (not just a local boolean) and persisted so it survives app reinstalls and works across the user's devices on the same store account.
- A "Restore Purchases" control exists in Settings — required for App Store review approval of any non-consumable IAP, and standard practice on Play as well (device/account switching).
- Purchase-gating fails safe: if purchase state hasn't loaded yet at launch (e.g. offline cold start), both the banner/interstitial ads AND the rewarded "skip interstitial" offer default to their pre-purchase (showing) state rather than silently hiding — never the reverse, to avoid revenue loss from indefinite false negatives.
- No regression to the existing interstitial timing (still fires once, after workout completion).

## Dependencies
- Requires adding an IAP layer — either `react-native-iap` (self-managed receipt validation) or a managed service like RevenueCat (handles cross-platform receipt validation and entitlement state out of the box). Neither is in package.json today.
- Requires App Store Connect and Google Play Console product configuration for the "Remove Ads" non-consumable, plus sandbox/test-track purchase testing on both platforms before submission.
- Requires a new rewarded ad unit ID created in the AdMob console (only banner + interstitial exist today in `src/config/adConfig.ts`).
- Play Store's declared ad content/rating may need review once a paid ad-removal path exists alongside ads — check Play Console's current app content requirements before submission.
